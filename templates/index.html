Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. The ovaries develop small fluid-filled sacs (follicles) and may fail to regularly release eggs. Early detection through ultrasound imaging combined with clinical assessment is critical for effective management.

Polycystic Ovaries

12+ follicles visible on ultrasound, typically 2–9 mm diameter.

Irregular Periods

Infrequent, irregular or prolonged menstrual cycles (>35 days).

Hormonal Imbalance

Excess androgens causing acne, hirsutism, and scalp hair thinning.

Metabolic Issues

Insulin resistance, weight gain, and elevated blood sugar risk.

Fertility Problems

Difficulty conceiving due to irregular or absent ovulation cycles.

Cardiovascular Risk

Elevated risk of hypertension, dyslipidemia, and heart disease.

Early detection matters. PCOS is manageable with lifestyle changes and medication when caught early. Regular ultrasound screening with AI-assisted analysis can dramatically improve detection rates and reduce the 70% who currently go undiagnosed.

Explainable AI

How the AI Explains Its Decision

Traditional deep learning models are "black boxes" — they give predictions without explanation. In a medical context, clinicians need to understand why a model made a decision before trusting it.

This tool offers 3 XAI methods you can choose from when analyzing an image:

  • Analytical CAM: Direct weight-to-feature-map mapping. Fast, saturation-free.
  • Saliency Map: Pixel-level gradient of the logit w.r.t. input pixels. Highlights fine texture.
  • Integrated Gradients: Accumulates gradients along the interpolation path from a blank baseline. Most theoretically rigorous.
  • All three use a logit-domain trick to bypass sigmoid saturation, ensuring valid heatmaps even for highly confident predictions.

CAM Pipeline — Step by Step

1
Preprocessing

Resize to 224×224, apply MobileNet normalization (−1 to +1).

2
Feature Extraction

MobileNet produces a 7×7×1024 spatial feature map.

3
Weight Mapping

Dense layer weights (50,176) reshaped to 7×7×1024 spatial form.

4
Contribution Score

Σ(feature × weight) per spatial cell → class-signed ReLU.

5
Heatmap Overlay

Upsampled to 224×224, JET colormap, blended 50/50 with original.

 Interpreting These Results

  • Red/yellow zones — regions with highest model attention. In PCOS cases these typically correspond to follicular clusters on the ovarian periphery.
  • Blue zones — contributed minimally. Usually areas outside the ovarian tissue or homogeneous background.
  • The confidence score reflects model certainty — near 100% means the model sees a clear signal. Lower scores indicate borderline cases that need extra clinical evaluation.
